# WriteCanvas Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 25, 2026

## Overview

WriteCanvas is a desktop application for handwritten notes, drawing, page organization, and document export.

## Information WriteCanvas processes

WriteCanvas reads and writes note files and folders that the user selects. It also saves application settings needed to remember the user's chosen library location. Notes may contain personal or sensitive information, depending on what the user writes or imports.

## Local storage and transmission

WriteCanvas processes notes, drawings, page data, settings, and exported documents on the user's device or in folders chosen by the user. The application does not operate an account system or a remote service for collecting note content, and it makes no network requests at runtime. The project does not include its own advertising, analytics, or telemetry service, and WriteCanvas does not send note content to the developer.

If the user chooses a folder managed by a separate synchronization or backup service, that service may process or transmit files under its own terms and privacy policy. The operating system and other software on the device are also governed by their respective providers' policies.

## Exporting and sharing

WriteCanvas lets users export their content, including to PDF, PNG, and the WriteCanvas project format. Exported files are saved to locations selected by the user. The user controls whether to share or upload those files elsewhere.

## Support

Users may contact the project through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ThanhNguyxnOrg/WriteCanvas/issues). Information voluntarily included in an issue or other public GitHub contribution may be visible to the public and is subject to GitHub's policies. Please do not post private notes, credentials, or other sensitive information in public issues.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as WriteCanvas changes. Updates will be published in this repository.

For privacy questions or support, visit [the WriteCanvas GitHub repository](https://github.com/ThanhNguyxnOrg/WriteCanvas) or open an issue at [WriteCanvas Issues](https://github.com/ThanhNguyxnOrg/WriteCanvas/issues).
